Greenland opened its new **Ilulissat International Airport** on Thursday 29 October, with a 2,200-metre runway that allows jets to fly directly from Europe to the town beside the UNESCO-listed Ilulissat Icefjord. The opening completes the three-airport programme of Greenland, part of the Kingdom of Denmark, after the enlarged airport at Nuuk in November 2024 and Qaqortoq in April 2026, according to [Airliner World](https://airlinerworld.com/greenland-opens-new-ilulissat-international-airport/), [Arctic Portal](https://arcticportal.org/news-events/news/4189-ilulissat-international-airport-opens-expanding-connections-to-north-greenland) and [Aviation A2Z](https://aviationa2z.com/index.php/2026/10/30/greenlands-new-ilulissat-airport-is-ready-for-jets-direct-flights/).

## From 845 metres to 2,200 metres

The new runway replaces a strip of about 845 metres, and larger passenger aircraft can now use the airport. A new passenger terminal of about 5,000 square metres comes with it. The old airfield has closed, Aviation A2Z reports.

{{figure:runway.svg}}

The first aircraft to use the new runway in airline service was an **Air Greenland Airbus A330-800**, OY-GKN, named Tuukkaq, which landed from Copenhagen on opening day, the first widebody service at Ilulissat, Airliner World writes. Aviation A2Z adds that an opening flight from Nuuk was flown by a three-person Air Greenland crew, all from Ilulissat, and that Greenland's Premier, Jens-Frederik Nielsen, attended the ceremony and told the town: "Today, the airport is yours."

## The schedule

Regular service to Copenhagen starts on **2 November 2026**, generally once a week, with a **Jettime Boeing 737-800** operating for Air Greenland, and extra flights around Christmas, New Year and March 2027. From **April 2027**, Air Greenland's Airbus A320neo OY-GRL, Unaaq, is due to take over the route, with up to six weekly rotations in the summer peak. **Icelandair** plans up to three weekly flights from Keflavík between 3 June and 16 October 2027, the sources say.

Until now, travellers to Ilulissat had to change in Nuuk or Kangerlussuaq and continue on a small domestic aircraft. Kangerlussuaq is losing its role as Greenland's main international gateway, with Nuuk as the main hub and Ilulissat as the second international airport, Aviation A2Z notes.

## Approved with temporary conditions

The Danish Civil Aviation and Railway Authority approved the airport after inspecting and testing its aviation safety systems, Aviation A2Z reports. The approval includes **temporary operating conditions for the initial phase**, and the operator, Greenland Airports, plans to extend opening hours and activity step by step, aiming for **full operations in summer 2027**. Arctic Portal confirms the gradual ramp-up and the summer 2027 target. None of the sources describes the airport's air traffic services or instrument approach procedures.

Greenland Airports was created in 2024 by merging Kalaallit Airports and Mittarfeqarfiit. Its chief executive, Jens Lauridsen, called the opening a historic milestone and said the three airports fundamentally change how Greenland connects internally and with the rest of the world, Airliner World reports.

The programme had its controversies. Under a 2018 agreement, the Danish state bought a one-third stake in the company building the Nuuk and Ilulissat airports for DKK 700 million and committed loans of up to DKK 450 million, an arrangement some Greenlandic politicians opposed, according to Aviation A2Z. The airport itself opened about two years later than first planned, after construction delays.

## Why it matters

A runway long enough for jets changes the operating picture well beyond tourism. Arctic Portal notes that Statbank Greenland recorded the highest international passenger numbers of its 2008–2025 series in 2025, with Nuuk rising sharply and Kangerlussuaq falling, even before the new airports opened. For air traffic services, three airports able to take larger aircraft, opened within two years, mean new procedures, new traffic patterns and a gradual build-up of operating hours that controllers, pilots and the airport operator have to learn together. The phased opening, with temporary conditions until full operations in summer 2027, reflects that.
