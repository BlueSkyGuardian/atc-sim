Morocco's aeronautics industry is growing faster than the country's exports as a whole. In the first nine months of 2026, the sector exported **more than 26.21 billion dirhams (MAD)** of goods, **up 22.3% year on year**, according to the latest monthly foreign trade bulletin of the Office des Changes, Morocco's foreign exchange office, as reported on 31 October by [Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/10/344538/moroccos-aeronautics-exports-jump-22-3-automotive-sector-grows-14-3/). Over the same period, total Moroccan exports rose by 10.7%, to MAD 384.05 billion.

## Assembly leads the way

The nine-month growth is driven above all by **assembly**, up **27.5%**, while **EWIS** (electrical wiring interconnection systems, the bundles of cables that run through an aircraft) grew by **12%**, according to Morocco World News. For comparison, the automotive sector, still the country's largest exporter, sold MAD 132.11 billion abroad over the same nine months (+14.3%), and phosphates and their derivatives MAD 74.65 billion (+0.2%).

The full-year picture for 2025, published in the Office des Changes' annual foreign trade report and detailed on 1 November by [Aéronautique.ma](https://www.aeronautique.ma/Aeronautique-les-exportations-marocaines-approchent-30-milliards-de-dirhams-en-2025_a6130.html), shows the same pattern. Aeronautics exports reached **MAD 29.93 billion in 2025**, up **9.8%** from MAD 27.26 billion in 2024 and well above the MAD 18.60 billion of 2019. That is about **6.4% of Morocco's merchandise exports**, which totalled MAD 470.05 billion in 2025. Aeronautics ranks behind automotive, phosphates and derivatives, agriculture and agri-food, and textiles and leather.

In 2025, assembly accounted for **MAD 19.95 billion** (+10.6%), about two thirds of the sector's exports, and EWIS for **MAD 9.87 billion** (+8.5%). "L'assemblage demeure le principal moteur de cette dynamique" ("assembly remains the main driver of this momentum"), Aéronautique.ma notes.

## Imports too

The trade runs both ways. Imports of "parts of aircraft and other air or space vehicles" reached MAD 17.26 billion in 2025, up 19%, of which **MAD 12.52 billion (72.5%) came from the United States** and MAD 3.58 billion (20.8%) from France. Exports in that same customs category totalled MAD 17.76 billion (+10.1%); Aéronautique.ma points out that this product classification differs from the scope used for the sector figures above.

Two cautions apply when comparing the numbers. The nine-month 2026 figures and the full-year 2025 figures come from different Office des Changes publications and cover different periods, so they cannot be added or set side by side as a trend. And from May 2026 the Office des Changes revised its sector data, after the Ministry of Industry and Commerce updated its lists of automotive and aeronautics operators.

## Why it matters

For readers of ATC SIM, this is industry rather than air traffic news, but the two are connected: a growing aeronautics sector is part of the same aviation economy as Morocco's airports and airlines, and it needs engineers, technicians and training. The full-year figure for 2026 will come with the Office des Changes' annual data.
