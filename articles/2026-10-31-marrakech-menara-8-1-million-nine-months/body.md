Marrakech-Menara airport (GMMX) handled **8,113,680 passengers** between January and September 2026, **9.88% more** than the 7,383,977 recorded in the same period of 2025, according to figures from the Office National des Aéroports (ONDA) published on 30 October by the official news agency MAP and carried by [Akhbarona](https://www.akhbarona.com/mobile/divers/434910.html), [Mamlaka Press](https://www.mamlakapress.com/archives/293749) and [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/494357-aeroport-marrakech-menara-hausse-de-pres-de-8-du-trafic-des-passagers-a-fin-septembre.html).

## September: still growing, a little more slowly

In September alone, the airport handled **877,833 passengers**, up **7.65%** from 815,468 a year earlier. International traffic, which makes up the vast majority of the airport's activity, reached 837,349 passengers (+7.13%), while domestic traffic rose much faster, by **19.69%**, to 40,484 passengers.

The domestic figure is small but the trend is clear over the nine months: 367,654 domestic passengers against 293,936 in 2025, an increase of **25.08%**. International passengers totalled 7,746,026 over the period (+9.25%).

Hespress's headline speaks of growth of "nearly 8%"; that is the September figure. The nine-month growth given in the body of the same article, and in the MAP dispatch, is 9.88%.

## Paris, London, Madrid

The five busiest international routes in September all link Marrakech with three European capitals:

1. **Paris-Orly**: 61,746 passengers (7.37% of international traffic)
2. **Paris-Charles de Gaulle**: 53,778 (6.42%)
3. **London Gatwick**: 37,480 (4.48%)
4. **Madrid**: 35,597 (4.25%)
5. **London Stansted**: 27,279 (3.26%)

Together, the two Paris airports account for nearly 14% of the airport's international passengers in the month, and the two London airports for almost 8%. The five routes combined represent a little under a quarter of the international traffic, which shows how widely the rest is spread across many European cities.

## What it means for controllers

For the approach and tower controllers at Marrakech, these numbers translate into a dense flow of medium-haul arrivals and departures from the north, most of them crossing Spanish and French airspace before entering the Casablanca flight information region. The fourth quarter, with the airlines' winter schedules that began on 25 October, will show whether the pace holds.

Across all Moroccan airports, ONDA counted **29,330,764 passengers** at the end of September, up 8.74% from 26,972,726 a year earlier (see our article of 28 October on the national figures). Marrakech alone therefore accounts for a little under 28% of the country's passengers.

## The capacity question

ONDA's "Airports 2030" plan, presented at the Marrakech Airshow earlier this month, sets a capacity target of **16 million passengers** for Marrakech-Menara, according to [Aeronautique.ma](https://www.aeronautique.ma/Marrakech-Airshow-2026-l-ONDA-met-en-scene-sa-transformation-sous-la-marque-Airports-of-Morocco_a6117.html); no completion date was given for that project.
