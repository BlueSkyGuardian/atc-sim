Royal Air Maroc (RAM) will fly its first scheduled direct service between Casablanca and Essaouira on **Sunday 1 November 2026**. The route, announced on 15 October and reported by [La Vie éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/casablanca-essaouira-ram-propose-un-aller-retour-a-800-dh/) and [LeBrief](https://www.lebrief.ma/royal-air-maroc-lance-une-ligne-directe-casablanca-essaouira-100169731/), runs twice a week, on Wednesdays and Sundays, under an agreement between the airline and the Marrakech-Safi region.

## The schedule

Both sources publish the same timetable (local times):

* **Sunday:** Casablanca 08:20 – Essaouira 09:40, then Essaouira 10:20 – Casablanca 11:40.
* **Wednesday:** Casablanca 16:00 – Essaouira 17:20, then Essaouira 18:00 – Casablanca 19:20.

The scheduled time is 1 hour 20 minutes in each direction, with a 40-minute turnaround at Essaouira. The airline sells the route at a fixed round-trip fare of 800 dirhams in economy and 1,600 dirhams in business class. Neither report names the aircraft type.

RAM presents the route as a way to connect Essaouira to its Casablanca hub, from which passengers can reach the airline's 86 international destinations. With this addition, La Vie éco counts 28 lines in the airline's domestic network.

## An airport that lives on international traffic

Essaouira-Mogador (ICAO: GMMI) is a small airport, but a growing one. According to figures from the Office National Des Aéroports (ONDA) reported by [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/461858-hausse-de-plus-de-34-du-trafic-de-passagers-en-2025-a-de-laeroport-essaouira-mogador.html), it handled **310,436 passengers in 2025**, up 34.4% from 230,959 in 2024, and **2,289 aircraft movements** (landings and take-offs), up 29.7% from 1,764.

{{figure:passengers.svg}}

What stands out is how little of that traffic is domestic. For January and February 2026, ONDA counted 42,088 passengers at Essaouira, of whom 41,918 were international and only **170 domestic**, a fall of 87.4% on the same months of 2025 ([Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/470529-onda-hausse-de-plus-de-10-du-trafic-de-passagers-a-laeroport-essaouira-mogador.html)). The airport recorded 352 movements over those two months. A scheduled link to Casablanca therefore fills a real gap: until now, a passenger from Essaouira heading for most of RAM's network had first to travel by road to another airport.

## What it means on the frequencies

For controllers, the numbers are modest but not negligible. Two rotations a week add four movements a week at Essaouira (two arrivals and two departures), and the same at Casablanca Mohammed V (GMMN). Measured against Essaouira's 2025 total, which works out at a little over six movements a day on average, that is a visible addition to a quiet aerodrome's traffic, and it brings a different kind of flight: a domestic sector between Morocco's main hub and the Atlantic coast, rather than the international arrivals that make up nearly all of GMMI's traffic today.

The Sunday morning rotation leaves Casablanca at 08:20; the Wednesday rotation operates in the late afternoon and lands back at Casablanca at 19:20. For trainees working Casablanca approach scenarios, it is one more domestic departure and arrival to sequence among the international flows.

## Context

The route comes at a time when Moroccan airports are growing quickly: ONDA's figures to the end of September, which this newsroom reported yesterday, show traffic up 8.74% in passengers and 9.70% in aircraft movements over nine months. The Casablanca–Essaouira link is small in that picture, but it ties one more regional airport directly to the national hub.

## What comes next

The first flight is scheduled for Sunday morning. The reports do not say how long the agreement with the Marrakech-Safi region runs or whether frequencies could increase; ONDA's monthly statistics for November, usually published the following month, will be the first indication of how many passengers the new domestic link carries at Essaouira.
