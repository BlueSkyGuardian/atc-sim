En-route radar training on the real Agadir sectors

A multi-seat simulator for air traffic controllers. The traffic flies the AIP points, airways and RAD routings of the Agadir CTA, with flight plans, OLDI coordination, storms that move and pilots who ask for deviations. Every exercise is recorded, rated and kept in your statistics.
