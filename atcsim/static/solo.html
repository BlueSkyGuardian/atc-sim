Solo radar

Pick a scenario and the radar opens with you as the controller: the same screen, labels, menus, MTCD, CPDLC and tools as in an exercise. Simulated pilots fly what you enter, read it back, call you and ask for what they need. Pause, speed up or end it from the control bar. Every solo run is kept in your exercises and statistics, with its recording.

Assume, then clearAircraft call you (CALL): click the callsign or press A. Enter clearances in the label menus or on the command line (Enter).
Pilots answerThe read-back, or "unable" with the reason, shows in the radio log at the bottom left. Their requests appear there with Approve / Unable.
Data linkConnected aircraft send requests by CPDLC and answer your uplinks by themselves.
